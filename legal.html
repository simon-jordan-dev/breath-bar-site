Privacy

Short version: Breath Bar has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your settings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Your settings, techniques and Pro license key are saved only on your device. We never see them.

When the app goes online

  • The time. To keep your devices in step, the app asks public time servers (time.cloudflare.com and pool.ntp.org) for the current time. Nothing about you is sent. You can turn this off with "Match my phone".
  • Updates. The Windows app checks GitHub for a newer version every few hours and downloads it from there.
  • Pro. When you enter a Pro license key, and now and then after that, the app checks the key with Lemon Squeezy. It sends the key and the name "Breath Bar on Windows", nothing else.

Like any website or download server, GitHub (which hosts this site and the downloads), the time servers and Lemon Squeezy see your IP address when the app or your browser connects to them. Their own privacy policies cover that.
