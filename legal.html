Privacy Refunds Terms

Privacy

Short version: Breath Bar has no accounts and no ads. It sends anonymous usage data, described below, and your settings stay on your device.

What stays on your device

Your settings, techniques and Pro license key are saved only on your device. We never see them.

When the app goes online

  • The time. To keep your devices in step, the app asks public time servers (time.cloudflare.com and pool.ntp.org) for the current time. Nothing about you is sent. On Windows you can turn this off with "Match my phone". On Android it only happens while "Match my PC" is on.
  • Updates. The Windows app checks GitHub for a newer version every few hours and downloads it from there. The Android app is updated through Google Play.
  • Pro. When you enter a Pro license key, and now and then after that, the app checks the key with our license server at api.breathbar.app, which runs on Cloudflare. It sends the key and the name "Breath Bar on Windows" or "Breath Bar on Android", nothing else.
  • Anonymous usage data. So we can see how Breath Bar is used and what to improve, the app sends usage data to PostHog, an analytics service, on its servers in the EU. Each install gets a random ID that isn't linked to you. The data covers: when the app starts and how many times a day; how long each day it runs, the bar is on screen and the settings are open, and which techniques and scenes were on screen for how long; the settings you choose, including colors, width, transparency, pace, your own Custom timings, sounds and shortcuts; the setup steps you go through; and Pro previews, purchases started and activations. It also includes the app version and language, plus the Windows version and number of screens on Windows, or the Android version on Android. It never includes your name, email, license key, what's on your screen or anything you type. Because the ID isn't linked to you, we can't tell which data is yours.

Like any website or download server, GitHub (which hosts this site and the downloads), Cloudflare (which runs our license server), the time servers, PostHog and Stripe see your IP address when the app or your browser connects to them. Their own privacy policies cover that.

This website

breathbar.app counts visits anonymously with PostHog, on its servers in the EU. It records that a visit happened, which parts of the page were seen, which buttons were clicked (such as Download or a bar on the page), how far down the page you scrolled and for how long, the site that linked you here, your screen size, device type and browser language. It sets no cookies and stores nothing in your browser, and the random ID it uses lasts only for that one page view, so visits can't be linked to each other or to you.

Buying Pro

On breathbar.app, Stripe sells Pro as the merchant of record, through its Link service. Stripe handles your payment, receipt and taxes, and shares your name, email address and country with us so we can help with your order. We never see your card details.

When you buy, our license server makes your license key and keeps it with your email address, your plan, Stripe's reference for your purchase, and the devices the key is switched on for (as "Breath Bar on Windows" or "Breath Bar on Android"). It emails you the key through Resend, an email service, and can send it again if you ask on the site. We use your email only for your purchase and support, never for marketing unless you ask for it.

In the Android app, you buy Pro through Google Play. Google handles your payment and subscription under its own terms and privacy policy, and we never see your card details. The app asks Google Play whether you have Pro, so it can unlock it.

Your rights

You can ask what we hold about you, or ask us to delete it, by emailing support@breathbar.app. We keep purchase records only as long as the law requires.

Refunds

If Pro isn't right for you, email support@breathbar.app within 14 days of your first payment, or of buying lifetime Pro, and you'll get a full refund. You don't need to give a reason. Your license key stops working once the refund goes through.

The same goes for Pro bought on Google Play: email us, or ask Google Play for a refund. Pro stops working once the refund goes through.

You can cancel a subscription at any time on breathbar.app/manage.html with your license key (no account needed), or at link.com with the email you bought with, or if you bought it on Google Play, from Subscriptions in the Play Store. Pro then keeps working until the end of the month or year you've paid for, and you won't be charged again. Later renewals aren't refunded, unless the law where you live says otherwise.

You can try every Pro option in the app for 3 minutes before you buy, so you know what you're getting.
