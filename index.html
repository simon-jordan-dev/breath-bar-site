Seeing a blue warning when you install?

Breath Bar is new and made by one person, so Windows doesn't recognise it yet. The first time you open the installer, Windows may show a blue box saying it "protected your PC". Click More info, then Run anyway. After that, Breath Bar installs and keeps itself up to date.
